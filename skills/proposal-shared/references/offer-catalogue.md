## 0. Rules for using this file

- **Currency: GBP. All prices ex VAT.** Say "ex VAT" wherever a price is shown.
- No USD list prices. If a client is billed in dollars, ask the user for the figure; never convert yourself.
- Round numbers only (no 999s, no pence).
- Prices go on a client file only when the profile says "Prices shown: yes". Otherwise scope only.
- **Default: price services one by one.** Use a bundle (section 5) only when the client wants one
  package or a bundle is clearly cheaper for them. Never show both on one pricing slide unless the user asks.
- "Quoted per ..." and "price to confirm" items: never write a number on a client file until the
  user gives one. Write "quoted per event" (or similar) instead.
- Performance terms (fee per booked call, CRM attribution): only when the user asks. The
  about £200 per booked call figure is floated, not agreed. Never put it on a client file unprompted.
- Portco pricing is agreed deal by deal with Jay or Dan. For a portco, ask the user; do not use list prices.
- Tools, ad spend, tickets, travel, venues, food and sponsorship are paid by the client directly
  at cost, never marked up, never in our fee. We cover lead data.

## 1. Structure (how to present the offer)

| Layer | Services | One line for a client |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Growth Console | The CRM, data and sending setup every channel runs on, and one live view of every touch, reply and meeting. Built in the client's name |
| Core | Outbound; Founder branding; LinkedIn ads; GTM System Build; Core add-ons (white papers, lead magnets, sales assets) | The channels most clients need from day one |
| Premium | Customer testimonials; Events; Regional dinners; AEO | Channels that build trust with bigger buyers |

Order on any services or plan slide: the base on top, then Core, then Premium. **Internal name only: never write "Growth Console" (or "the console") in a client deck or file.** It is included in everything; describe what it does (the CRM, data and sending set up in their name, one live view of every touch, reply and meeting). The deck gate fails the name.
Most clients start with one or two services. Nothing goes live until the client signs it off.
First 6 weeks: about 80% of the work is experiments to find the channels that work; with
enterprise buyers allow 2 to 3 months to prove results.

## 2. Base

### Growth Console (internal name, never in client copy)
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Growth Console | No separate list price. Part of every engagement. Confirm with the user before quoting it on its own |

Scope: CRM set up or rebuilt (lifecycle stages, lead source, dedupe, pipelines, routing, reply
alerts, data migration); CRM refresh and reactivation; sending setup (domains, inboxes,
warm-up, LinkedIn senders, daily health checks); live console (every touch logged by channel,
sender, campaign and date; funnel from contacted to pipeline, per service and in total).
Built by our GTM engineers in the first 2 weeks. Client buys tools in its own name.
Metric definitions: reply rate = replies / people actually messaged; positive reply = any
interest; prospects per positive reply = unique people contacted / positive replies; net
conversion rate = meetings booked (or event attendees) / unique people contacted; meeting =
booking or meeting tag, once per person; show rate = held / (held + no-shows). Meeting credit
goes to the last campaign touching the person in the prior 90 days; a referral overrides.

## 3. Core

### Outbound (per month, 3-month minimum)
| Option | GBP / month ex VAT | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | £2,000 | 2 to 3 sender accounts, 2 to 3 signals, warm calls on replies, weekly report, monthly review |
| LinkedIn and email | £4,000 | LinkedIn, plus signal-based and account-based email, up to 30,000 emails a month |
| LinkedIn, email and calling | £5,000 | LinkedIn and email, plus cold calling by our team to Tier 1 leads |

Scope notes: strategy (product, offer, ICP, personas, signals, market sizing shown before we
start); data (accounts and leads qualified and scored, LinkedIn activity checked, emails
validated, tiers with per-company caps, phones for Tier 1, dedupe against CRM and do-not-contact,
client reviews a pilot of 10 to 20 accounts first); outreach (one-to-one for top accounts incl.
founder messages, personal video, gifts for the top 3; one-to-few; one-to-many; lookalikes;
signal-based; founder's first-degree network; reasons to talk such as event invites and
reports); copy per tier and persona, 2 to 3 variants, no links or "quick chat" in a first
message, client approves every message; inbox and reply management, meetings booked, no-show
recovery, CRM updated. Client provides LinkedIn accounts, secondary domains and inboxes,
sending tools, CRM access, sign-off. We provide the data and run everything else.
Signal library: `research-signals.md`.

### Founder branding (per month, 3-month minimum, one founder per package)
| Option | GBP / month ex VAT | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | £2,000 | 1 founder, 8 to 12 posts a month |
| Plus | £3,500 | 1 founder, 12 to 15+ posts a month, plus the quarterly video shoot (about 3 long videos and 12 clips a quarter) |

Both include: research (founder's last 30 posts or 6 months, benchmark of 20 to 35 founders,
4 in depth, gaps nobody covers); plan (positioning line, 4 to 5 pillars, formats, cadence; mix
about 40% reach, 40% trust, 20% conversion, soft asks only); posts drafted two weeks ahead from
the founder's own calls; profile rebuild (headline, banner, About, Featured); engagement (DMs to
ICP people who connect, follow, view or engage, then into outbound; blank connection requests to
ICP); reactive posts on request; monthly brainstorm and retrospective; monthly reporting on ICP
reach, not likes. Video is Plus only. No commenting on others' posts on the founder's behalf
unless agreed in writing. Nothing goes live without the founder's sign-off. Founder time: one
brainstorm and one retrospective a month, one sitting a week to approve drafts. A second founder
is a second package.

### Events
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Events | Quoted per event. Tickets, travel and sponsorship paid by the client at cost |

conferences: about two weeks of work per event (side events mapped, scored lists of people to
meet, copy approved by the founder, meeting requests sent, live shared calendar, follow-ups the

### Regional dinners
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Regional dinners | Quoted per dinner. Venue and food paid by the client at cost |

Our job: get the right people to register and attend. We build the invite list (15 to 20 target
buyers plus 2 to 3 of the client's customers or design partners), run about two weeks of
outreach from the client's LinkedIn and email, call non-repliers in the final week, track
registrations, research attendees for seating, and follow up everyone the day after.
The client's job: set the date, city, venue, budget and event page, and **run the night**
(the founder hosts). About 3 to 4 weeks from start to the night. Do not say we run or host the
dinner itself.

## 6. Terms

- 3-month minimum on every monthly service.
- Setup fee £2,000 ex VAT, usually waived for a pilot. Ask the user whether to show it.
- Client pays tools, ad spend and event costs directly at cost. We cover lead data.
- Aim: the client can run its own GTM infrastructure and outbound in 6 to 18 months. We are a growth partner, not a dependency.

## 7. Fit and exclusions (check before proposing)

Good fit: AI B2B SaaS with high ACV; B2B services only for exceptional or network founders
(specialist recruiters, top dev shops, industrial automation, high-value consulting); market not
heavily commoditised; HQ in the UK or US (buyers anywhere); at least 20,000 contactable people;
a real market opportunity.
Not a fit (flag to the user, do not build): commoditised services (marketing agencies, coaching,
LinkedIn agencies), competitors (GTM, LinkedIn, automation agencies), customer lifetime value
under £10k (portcos excepted), pre-launch, very small TAM, a competitor of a Blissgrowth portco.
We do not do: website builds, design-only work, unedited AI content, Google or Meta ads.
