# Confirmation rounds

How to turn the extracted profile into the fewest, sharpest questions.

## What each block covers

| Block | Fields | Usually a question when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Company and what they sell | what they sell, to whom, stage, funding, size, HQ, website | stage or funding differs between sources; product description is vague |
| 2. People and who decides | names, roles, decision role, LinkedIn, whose founder brand is in scope | nobody is clearly the decision maker; a co-founder or exec was mentioned without a role |
| 3. Discussed and promised | prices said out loud, pilot or trial terms, timelines, guarantees, names promised | a number was said informally; a promise was made by someone not on the call |
| 4. Their market | buyer titles, industries, company size, regions, exclusions, deal size, hard vs soft | titles are ambiguous (CPO, CRO, CDO); size or region was not stated; deal size unknown |
| 5. Our scope | services in scope and lead service, deadline and presenter, prices shown, format, sensitivities | always confirm services and prices shown, even if sources suggest them |

Ambiguous titles: write them out in full before asking ("Chief Procurement Officer", not
"CPO"), because the short forms also match unrelated roles and wreck market counts later.

## Format of a round

One message. Accepted facts first, compact. Then numbered questions per block. Each question
ends with the suggested answer in brackets and where it came from.

## Example round 1

```
Here's what I understood about Atmospheric AI (from 4 sources: the 18 Nov call, Jay's brief,
Dan's Slack notes, the website). Say "all good" or correct by number.

Taken from sources (shout if anything is wrong)
- Sells AI for procurement teams at large manufacturers. Raw materials and packaging focus.
- London based. Roee Goldberg is co-founder and CEO.
- Sells founder-led today, with an ex-Bain CCO.

1. Company
 1. Stage: Jay's brief says pre-PMF; the call says about $10m raised. Both true? [both: pre-PMF, about $10m raised]
 2. Rebrand by year end, so no AEO or website work in this proposal? [yes, from Jay's brief]

2. People
 3. Decision maker is Roee, and founder branding would be for Roee only? [yes]
 4. Should the CCO appear in the proposal at all? [no, default]

3. Discussed and promised
 5. Dan said on the call: "you can add and subtract channels as and when you see fit". Keep that as a term? [yes]
 6. Any prices said out loud? [none found in sources]

4. Their market
 7. Buyers: Chief Procurement Officer, VP Procurement, Head of Procurement, plus CFOs as the
    economic buyer? [from the call]
 8. Manufacturers above 1,000 employees in the UK, US and DACH: hard filter? [hard, default]
 9. Pharma as a separate segment with its own messaging? [yes, from Dan's notes]

5. Our scope
10. Services: signal-based outbound (lead), founder branding, LinkedIn ads, a procurement
    leaders dinner. [from the call]
11. Prices shown in the deck? [no, default]
12. Deck needed by? [no date found]
```

Twelve questions in one message, most answered with "yes". That is the target and the cap.

Question 11 (prices shown) is asked every time. Nothing the user already sent in writing is asked
again. For a redo, add a side-by-side block before the questions:

```
Earlier offer (Sep 2026)          New offer (proposed)
Outbound, Core                    Outbound, Core (lead)
Founder branding, Standard        Founder branding, Plus
Prices shown: yes                 Prices shown: ? [no, default]
```

## Round 2 and 3

Only what round 1 left open, plus anything the answers raised (for example, a new person was
named). Keep the same numbering style. After round 3, anything left goes into `gaps` with who
can fill it.

## What not to ask

- Anything a source states clearly and no other source contradicts. Show it, don't ask it.
- Anything only research can answer (follower counts, competitors' ads, market counts). Those
  go to proposal-research.
- Nice-to-know items that would not change the proposal.
