# offer.md template

`./proposals/<slug>/offer.md` is the agreed strategy and offer. proposal-deck reads it before it
writes a word and will not start without it, so it must hold everything a slide might need to
explain WHY this plan fits this client. Internal file: sources and the decision log stay here and
never go on a slide.

Copy from the line below.

---

# <Client>: offer and strategy
Agreed with <user> on <YYYY-MM-DD>. Profile version <n>, catalogue <v>.

## 1. Strategy brief
- **Audience**: who will read or hear the proposal (name, role, decision maker or not).
- **The decision we want**: what they say yes to at the end ("a 3-month outbound and founder
  branding plan starting in January").
- **Delivery**: live pitch on <date> presented by <name>, or a pre-read sent on <date>.
- **Core problem** (one sentence, sourced): why they are not at their goal yet.
- **Key messages** (3 to 5, each a full sentence the deck must land):
  1. ...
- **Proof available** (sourced, and whether it may be shown): their customers, results, quotes,
  awards; our neutral case evidence.
- **Density**: dense pre-read or lighter live deck.

## 3. Services and tests
For each: service (catalogue name), what we run, hypothesis, the one metric, success bar and
time box, what we need from them, start week.

## 4. Goals, stated precisely
| Goal | What is counted | Currency | By when | Whose (theirs or our estimate) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| £1m ARR from event organisers | annual recurring revenue from organiser contracts | GBP | Dec 2027 | theirs | call 05:20 |

## 5. Commercials
- Structure: base, core, premium, bundle (never name "Growth Console" on a client file; describe
  what it does).
- Prices shown in the deck: yes or no. If yes, the lines and totals from the catalogue, ex VAT.
- Pricing overrides and why. Verbal promises already made, word for word with source.
- Term, start date, review points.

## 6. Format and deadline
Outputs (deck, plan pages, one-pager), ground preference if known, deadline, who presents.

## 7. Questions to raise on the call
Anything still open that the client must answer (carried from the profile).
