# Test tracks

Where options come from. Work through every track for the client, keep what the evidence
supports, and drop what would read the same for any client. Services and prices live in
`../../proposal-shared/references/offer-catalogue.md`; this file is about what to test and how
to measure it.

## What makes a good option

- **Tied to the core problem.** If the problem is reach, an option that improves conversion on a
  page nobody visits is not first.
- **Sourced.** The "why it fits" line points at the profile, a call timestamp or a research file.
  "Most B2B companies benefit from..." is not a reason.
- **Small and time boxed.** A batch of 150 to 300 people, 4 to 6 weeks, one metric, one bar.
- **Something we can run.** It maps to a catalogue service, or to an add-on we quote.
- **Honest about what we need.** Their time, approvals, assets, access, a person on camera.

## The tracks

| Track | Questions to ask of the research | Typical options |
|---|---|---|
| Markets and regions | Where are their current customers? Which regions have the same buyer and no incumbent? What does the market map count per region? | UK first, then DACH; US once the UK play reads out; a city cluster for dinners |
| Buyer types | Who signs, who uses, who blocks? Are there titles they have not tried (finance, operations, a new hire in role)? | Economic buyer track, champion track, new-in-role track |
| Adjacent industries | Which industries look like their best customers (same problem, same buyer)? Is there any proof there yet? | Lookalikes of a named customer; a vertical tested by calls first |
| Channels | Which channels work for them today, which sit idle (warmed inboxes, a founder profile with followers)? | LinkedIn from founder and team senders, email in small batches, cold calling, InMail |
| Offers and hooks | What has converted best so far? What can they give away that costs them little? | A personalised page or teardown, a benchmark, a free audit, an invite |
| Partnerships | Who already sells to their buyer (consultancies, recruiters, platforms, associations)? | Co-marketing, referral partners, a co-hosted event |
| Founder branding | Does the founder have a voice the market trusts? Followers, post rhythm, top posts? | Core or Plus package, a specific content angle the competitors do not own |
| LinkedIn ads | Do competitors advertise (Ad Library)? Is the list small and high value? | Thought-leader ads to the target list, document ads for a report, retargeting |
| Events and dinners | Do their buyers gather at known events? Do customers and investors host well? | Event tiers (test, host, headline), a regional dinner series |
| Proof and testimonials | Which customers have a hard number and would go on camera? | One shoot day per story, lookalike campaigns led by the story |
| AI search | When buyers ask an AI assistant about the problem, is the client named? | AEO audit, answer pages, citation sources |
| Signals | Which buying signals exist for this market (`research-signals.md`)? | Hiring, new in role, funding, events attended, tech change |

## The one metric, by channel

| Channel or play | The one metric | Typical bar for a 4 to 6 week test |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn outreach | Positive replies per 100 messaged (positive reply = any interest) | 3 to 6 per 100 |
| Email | Positive replies per 100 delivered | 1 to 3 per 100 |
| Cold calling | Conversations per 100 dials, then meetings | 8 to 15 conversations, 1 to 3 meetings |
| Personalised page or asset | Replies or meetings from people who opened it | Set from their own history |
| Founder branding | Inbound conversations from the target list per month | 3 to 8 after month 2 |
| LinkedIn ads | Engaged target accounts, then meetings from them | Engaged accounts per 100 targeted |
| Events and dinners | Target-list attendees, then meetings within 30 days | Seats filled by named target accounts |
| AI search | Answers that name the client, over a fixed question set | Count per month against a baseline |

Bars are starting points: replace them with the client's own numbers when the profile has them,
and say where each bar came from. Reply rate is over people messaged, not over connections.

## Scoring

Score each option 1 to 5 on four things and add them up: fit to the core problem, strength of
the evidence, speed to a readable signal, and cost to the client (5 = cheapest). Ties go to the
option that teaches us the most. Show the score but let the conversation decide.

## Common traps

- Ten channels at once. A proposal with everything has no strategy.
- Testing a market with no proof and no cheap way to learn (start with calls or a small list).
- Goals with no currency or date ("1M ARR"), or targets that are ours but read as theirs.
- Options that need a founder's time the founder said they do not have.
- Copying the last client's tests. Reusing a format is fine; the substance must come from this
  client's research.
