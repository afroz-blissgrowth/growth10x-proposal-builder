# Content standard: what a full slide holds

Derived from the densest slides of the approved ContextPath deck (aeo 212 words and 90% fill,
dinners 192 words and 88%, ads 144 words and 86%, engine 214 words and 84%, ways 390 words with
flip cards) against the failed deck (108 words a slide, 82px empty bands, 0 count-ups, interaction
on 3 of 20 slides). A full slide is not a crowded slide: it has a claim, the mechanism behind it,
proof from the client's world, and something that moves or opens.

## Every content slide

| Element | Standard | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Action title | A claim with a verb, specific to the client, one highlighted phrase | name-swap test (critic) |
| Words | 130 to 220 visible words, unless the plan gives a reason and a lower range | `words` (deck_check) |
| Client substance | 3 or more client-specific facts: their names, customers, segments, competitors, numbers, places, their own words; each traced to a source in the plan | `specific`, `facts` |
| Mechanism | How it works, not only what it is: steps, lanes, a loop, inputs to outputs | critic |
| Proof | One number, quote or named result from their world (or neutral case evidence) | critic |
| Fill | Aim for 66% or more of the content box and content ending 40px or less above the footer; errors under 58% or over 70px. Deck averages: 70% fill, 30px band, 140 words (approved reference: 73%, 28px, 155) | `fill`, `empty-band`, deck averages |
| Motion | Header and every panel enter staggered; count-up on hero numbers; timelines draw | `motion`, `countup`, `timeline` |
| Interaction | At least one click or build in every run of 3 content slides; more on mechanism slides | `interaction` |
| Hierarchy | Card headings at least 1.2x the detail size and 200 heavier (row titles under 26px: 1.08x and 200 heavier); labels in caps | `hierarchy` |
| Units | Every k, m, bn and % names what it counts; money has a currency and a date | `units` |

## Per slide type (layout ids from layout-library.md)

### Goals (`stat-row`)
Must have: 2 to 4 goals stated precisely (what, currency, by when, whose), where they are today
for each, the gap in one number, the path as dated points, their own words about the goal.
From the ContextPath numbers slide: three stat cards hover-linked to a month timeline, plus a
proof strip on a build. 120 to 190 words.

### Situation (`situation`)
Must have: what is proven (named customers and numbers), what is idle or missing (named), the
gap card, one quote. Never imply they have not tried something; say what we add. 140 to 200 words.

### Market (`market-map`, `share`)
Must have: counts by segment and region with tool and date, explicit titles, what is excluded,
where we start and why. Count-ups on the counts. From the ContextPath prize slide: TAM, counts,
a ranked short list with scores, source line. 120 to 200 words.

### Plan (`plan`)
Must have: every play in priority order with one client-specific line on why, "our pick" for
the lead, every row jumping to its slide. 110 to 180 words.

### Mechanism (`process`, `lanes`)
Must have: the inputs (signals named for this market, 8 to 15 across 2 to 3 groups as on the
ContextPath engine slide), the steps, the tiers or lanes with who qualifies, what each tier gets,
the always-on rules (exclusions, approvals), and a build or popovers that walk through it.
150 to 220 words.

### Channel play (`channel-play`)
Must have, as on the ContextPath ads and AEO slides: who sees it (their audience, named lists),
what they see (formats, with the client's content), what happens next (qualification, hand-off,
tracking), a loop or flow diagram, and a mockup or tabs showing the real thing. 150 to 220 words.

### Events and dinners (`timeline`, `channel-play`)
Must have, as on the ContextPath dinners and pe-rooms slides: how it works (cities, size,
co-hosts), who we invite (named groups), proof (a past event with numbers, or the plan for the
first one), dated points that draw, what we do and what the client does. 160 to 220 words.

### Ways in (`flip-grid`, `tabs`)
Must have: 4 to 8 routes (partners, channels), each with a front (what and why for them) and a
back (how it works, first step). Words can reach 320 counting backs.

### Proof and stories (`proof`)
Must have: the story's numbers with sources, the customer's words, how one story becomes a
campaign, the lookalike profile. Mockup opener to the full example. 110 to 180 words.

### Roadmap (`timeline`)
Must have: dated milestones per month or quarter, what goes live, the review points, always-on
lanes. One main timeline per deck; others are short and different in form. 120 to 200 words.

### Commercials (`compare` or packages)
Must have: structure, what each part includes, prices only if shown (catalogue, ex VAT, round),
the term and review points. Never name "Growth Console". 110 to 190 words.

### Owners (`owners`), Ask (`ask`), Cover, Thanks
Owners: roles not names, both sides, what each side approves. Ask: kind `closing`, dated steps,
one question. Cover and thanks: the client's real logo, one highlighted line.

## When a slide fails fill or words

Add substance, not padding: the proof line, the mechanism step that was skipped, the named
segment, the source line, a build revealing the detail. Never stretch empty boxes to pass the
fill gate; the critic checks for hollow panels.
