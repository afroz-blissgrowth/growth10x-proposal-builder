# Storyline beats and service moves

Inspiration for the storyline (Stage 1) and the slide plan (Stage 2). This is a menu, not a
template: the storyline comes from `offer.md` (the agreed strategy, key messages and goals), and
each beat is written for this client. Approved decks (ContextPath, Tipped) show the depth to aim
for; their structure may be reused, their substance never stands in for this client's.

## Beats most decks need

| Beat | Job | Usually built as (layout id) | Needs from offer.md and the profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook (cover) | The promise in their terms, their logo | cover | Key message 1, prepared for, month |
| Situation | Where they are: what is proven, what is idle, the gap | situation | Proof, channels, capacity (profile s4 to s7) |
| Goal | Their goals stated precisely, the path, the gap in one number | stat-row | Goals table (offer.md s4) |
| Why this approach | The argument from their market, before the plan | thesis | Core problem, key messages |
| Market | Who we reach: segments, counts, explicit titles, where we start | market-map or share | Market map (profile s8), tool and date |
| Plan | The plays in priority order, each row jumping to its slide | plan | Strategy (offer.md s2) |
| Each play | Mechanism, who, what they see, proof, a mockup | process, lanes, channel-play | Services and tests (offer.md s3) |
| Measurement | What is tracked and how it is counted (never name the product) | compare or process | Metrics and success bars |
| First weeks | Dated milestones, review points, approvals | timeline | Start date, tests, time boxes |
| Commercials | Structure and, if shown, prices ex VAT | compare | Commercials (offer.md s5) |
| Owners | Roles on both sides, approvals | owners | People (profile s4) |
| Ask | Dated next steps and one closing question | ask | Decision we want |
| Thanks | Lockup with logo, one highlighted line | thanks | |

Skip a beat when there is nothing true and specific to put on it; never pad. No divider slides.
One main timeline (goals may show a short path; the roadmap carries the detail, in a different form).

## Service moves (what a play's slides usually need to show)

| Service | Moves that made approved decks land |
|---|---|
| Outbound | The signals for THIS market (named, 8 to 15 in 2 to 3 groups), scoring and tiers with who qualifies, what each tier gets, after-a-reply workflow, campaign list opening brief mockups, exclusions |
| Founder branding | Their founder's current presence (followers, rhythm, top posts from research), the category they can own and who already owns nearby ones, pillars with shares, how a post is made from their calls, package scope |
| LinkedIn ads | Who sees them (their named lists), what they see (their content as ad formats), the loop with outbound, what competitors run (Ad Library) |
| GTM System Build | The build list in their tools, the 30-day handover |
| Customer testimonials | Which customers, the hard numbers, one shoot day to many outputs, story to lookalike campaign |
| Events | Their buyers' rooms with dates and places, tiers (test, host, headline), budget line if shown |
| Regional dinners | Cities from their customer and investor map, co-hosts, who we invite, proof from a past event; we get the right people to register and attend, the client runs the night |
| AEO | The questions their buyers ask an AI, who gets named today, the answer pages we build, monthly tracking |

Scope lines and prices only from `../../proposal-shared/references/offer-catalogue.md`; prices
only if `offer.md` says they are shown, with "ex VAT" on the slide.

## Headline rules

- Action title: sentence case, one line at 64px (about 45 characters), no full stop, a claim
  with a verb, one highlighted phrase (usually the payoff).
- Eyebrow gives place in the deck: "Play 1 · Signal-based outbound", "Where you are today".
- Second person: "your test lab", not "Northwind's test lab".
